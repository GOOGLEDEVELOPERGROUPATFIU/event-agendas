## 🎤 Tech Talk #1

### Designing for Everyone: Building Accessible, High-Performance Web Experiences

**Time:** 11:00 AM – 11:40 AM  
**Hosted by:** Judith Rohatiner

This keynote session demystifies the core principles of web accessibility and why it matters for every developer. Through real-world examples, hands-on best practices, and insights from WCAG and assistive technologies, Judith Rohatiner explores how to build inclusive, high-performance websites that are perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust for all users — including those with disabilities.

Attendees will leave with actionable strategies to integrate accessibility into UI design, HTML structure, ARIA patterns, and everyday coding workflows.

**Q&A:** 11:40 AM – 11:50 AM

---

## 🎤 Tech Talk #2

### Using Passion for Community & Kubernetes to Unlock Emerging Markets

**Time:** 11:50 AM – 12:40 PM  
**Hosted by:** Angel Ramirez

This session explores how passion for open-source communities and Kubernetes can unlock opportunities in emerging markets. Angel Ramirez shares real-world lessons from building a global business through community leadership, innovation, and open collaboration.

**Q&A:** 12:40 PM – 12:50 PM

## 🎤 Tech Talk #3

### Building Your First AI Agent: Practical No-Code Automation Using n8n and Modern LLM Tools

**Time:** 12:50 PM – 1:30 PM  
**Hosted by:** Vlad Budichenko

**Workshop Resources:**  
https://guiltless-feels-27d.notion.site/DevFest-2025-2b382256953e80deab7cc3e70d359371

In this hands-on workshop, Vlad Budichenko, Founder of Vocaly AI and Senior Software Engineer, guides participants through building their first AI agent using accessible no-code tools.

Attendees will learn how modern AI agents work under the hood—from state-based components to decision flows—and then implement a real agent using n8n, LLMs, and API integrations.

This session focuses on practical, real-world automation patterns used by startups and engineering teams to build voice agents, workflow bots, and customer engagement tools. No prior AI or cloud experience is required, making it ideal for beginners, students, and developers looking to rapidly prototype intelligent agents with minimal setup.

## 🎤 Tech Talk #4

### The Future of AI: Multi-Agent Systems, LLM Reasoning, and Real-World Scientific Automation

**Time:** 2:00 PM – 2:40 PM  
**Hosted by:** Henry Ruiz

In this session, Henry Ruiz—Research Scientist at Texas A&M AgriLife Research and Google Developer Expert in AI and Google Cloud—will explore how next-generation AI, powered by multi-agent collaboration and advanced LLM reasoning, is transforming scientific research and real-world decision-making.

Henry will demonstrate how intelligent agents can coordinate and leverage multimodal capabilities to automate complex analytical workflows across diverse scientific domains.

Students will gain a practical understanding of how agentic AI systems work under the hood, why they are becoming essential to modern scientific workflows, and how beginners can start building the next generation of multi-agent applications using accessible frameworks such as Google’s ADK.

**Q&A:** 2:40 PM – 2:50 PM

---

## 🎤 Tech Talk #5

### Thinking With AI: How Emerging Tools Are Changing the Way We Learn

**Time:** 2:50 PM – 3:30 PM  
**Hosted by:** Kumani Riley

This talk explores how AI tools are reshaping modern learning and skill-building.

Kumani shares practical ways people can use AI to think more clearly, learn faster, and solve problems in a smarter way. The focus is on using AI intentionally rather than depending on it, and understanding how these tools can support human ideas instead of replacing them.

This session is designed for students, developers, and anyone interested in how technology is transforming the way we learn and create.

**Q&A:** 3:30 PM – 3:40 PM
